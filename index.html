Architecture Conformance Disclosure

The data model separates patient demographics (Patient), clinical encounters (Encounter), lab measurements (Observation with LOINC codes), and prescribed therapies (MedicationStatement with RxNorm / generic mapping). All extractions maintain provenance records documenting the original file, page number, confidence score, and clinician verification status.

Notice: This implementation is FHIR R4-inspired for hackathon demonstration. Live ABDM/M1/M2 production milestones require authorized gateway keys and Indian regulatory sandbox onboarding.
